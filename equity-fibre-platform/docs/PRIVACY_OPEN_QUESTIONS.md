# Privacy open questions

Cross-referenced to `ASSUMPTIONS_AND_OPEN_QUESTIONS.md`.

1. **Chorus data (Q9):** Will Chorus send customer contact info, only eligible addresses, referral codes, or no lead data? This determines whether indirect-collection notices are required.
2. **Authority & notice for Chorus data (Q10):** What legal basis and privacy notice governs any data Chorus transfers to Wireless Nation? Do not assume a list of ~10,000 households grants permission to market to or contact them.
3. **Evidence retention (Q44):** What are the approved retention periods for eligibility evidence (CSC/MSD documents)?
4. **Financial retention (Q45):** Which records must be retained for financial/contractual purposes, and for how long?
5. **Offshore access (Q40):** Exactly what data may an offshore support worker access?
6. **Offshore location (Q41):** Will offshore support operate from Myanmar (or elsewhere)? Adequacy assessment needed.
7. **Offshore contracts (Q42):** What contractual and privacy protections will govern offshore access?
8. **Privacy officer (Q43):** Who is Wireless Nation's privacy officer (accountable person)?
9. **Legal approval (Q46):** Who approves the privacy statement and customer terms?
10. **AI processing:** If Anthropic mode is enabled, confirm data-handling terms and that only redacted, approved content is sent. Eligibility evidence must never be sent to an AI provider.
11. **Marketing vs service consent:** Confirmed separated in-product; confirm wording and records meet legal requirements.
12. **Deletion vs legal hold:** Confirm which deletion requests can be honoured vs must be retained (e.g. financial records).

None of these block the demo; all are surfaced here and in the assumptions register so they can be resolved before launch.
